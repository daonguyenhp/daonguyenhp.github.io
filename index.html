About

Learning to build the system around the model

I study computer science in the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering at HCMUT. The part I am going deeper on is deep learning: computer vision and language models.

A notebook that prints an accuracy is the easy part. The harder part is the boundary: the web request must not hang on a tool, a log must rebuild the conversation, and a sentence in a design must survive the original paper.

I want to collaborate on open-source AI projects. I code better with EDM on.

  1. Draw the boundary first In Celesnity, the API, the loop, the LLM provider, and the outside world do not share one function.
  2. Check it, then keep it The HCMC flood report drops claims the 2021–2026 literature does not support.
  3. Write down what is missing Every write-up has a section for what is true. Work that is still moving also says what comes next.
