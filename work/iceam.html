Context

A shop assistant that only chats is not useful if it cannot see what is in stock. The project keeps the stock in a spreadsheet and lets the model answer directly from that file.

What it does

  • Node.js reads live inventory and product rows from Excel.
  • Customer questions go to a language model through OpenRouter grounded on the spreadsheet context.
  • Won Third Prize in the AI in Business competition (view award details and proof).

What is true

This is team work from May 2025, recognized with Third Prize in the AI in Business competition. The GitHub repository was published in Jan 2026. There is no live store deployment count beyond the competition and course evaluation.
