About & Contact

Learning to build the system around the model

I study computer science in the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering at HCMUT. My primary focus is deep learning across computer vision and language models.

A notebook that prints an accuracy score is the easy part. The harder part is the system boundary: the web request must not hang on a slow tool, an event log must rebuild the conversation state, and every design claim must survive comparison against the literature.

I am looking to collaborate on open-source AI systems. I code best with EDM on.

  1. Competitive gaming & esports Unwinding after lab hours with tactical team matches in Valorant and League of Legends: Wild Rift (Tốc Chiến), plus organizing campus esports tournaments like eCSE Cup.
  2. Music while coding & debugging Deep-focus playlists and EDM tracks stay on loop through late-night C++, ROS 2 simulation runs, and hackathon sprints.
  3. Reading & recharging Taking a break from screens with novels, manga, and light stories to reset between exam seasons and Youth Union campaigns.
